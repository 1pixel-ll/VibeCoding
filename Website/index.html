Where I come from

I'm based in Guwahati, Assam — grew up watching the Brahmaputra from the wrong side of a textbook. Most of the last two years went into entrance exams: JEE Main, Assam CEE, IISER IAT, NERIST NEE‑2, NSAT — the whole circuit. Somewhere between mock tests I found more excitement in writing scripts and editing video than in solving one more mechanics problem, and that's the part that stuck.

Outside of coursework I'm self-taught in video editing and 3D modelling, comfortable in Python, and currently building a small business around reusable AI chatbot products. Gaming is where I unwind — and where most of the clips above come from.
